Three strategic directions. Which orbit do you want to explore?

What wins Golden Nica (Interactive Art +)

The jury rewards work that changes conditions for others, not just work that is technically brilliant in isolation. Recent winners: Forensic Architecture (counter-forensics as public infrastructure), Lauren Lee McCarthy (social algorithms made visible through lived performance), Tega Brain & Julian Oliver (environmental sensing as governance). The pattern: the work must be a tool that others wield, not a monument they admire.

Direction A
THE OPEN LEDGER
"The terminal becomes a public audit infrastructure. Anyone can submit a river. Anyone can run a permit ruling. The thermodynamic verdicts accumulate into a planetary ledger that no government issued and no corporation controls."

What changes

  • Real hydrological data ingestion (API or manual)
  • Public, immutable verdict archive (CAS)
  • Verdicts become citable counter-evidence
  • Community-submitted kernel parameters

Ars argument

  • Forensic Architecture lineage (counter-forensics)
  • Tool others wield, not monument to admire
  • Real political utility beyond gallery
  • Scales: 1 river or 10,000 rivers

Direction B
THE INSTALLATION BODY
"The terminal leaves the browser and enters physical space. A room-scale installation where the visitor IS the thermodynamic input. Their body heat, their breath CO2, their movement entropy become kernel parameters. The room audits the visitor."

What changes

  • Sensor array: temperature, CO2, motion, sound
  • Physical terminal (CRT or projection)
  • Visitor biometrics as kernel input parameters
  • Room-scale Gray-Scott on walls/floor

Ars argument

  • Embodied interaction (not just screen)
  • Visitor confronted with their own thermodynamic cost
  • Spectacular for jury walkthroughs

Direction C
THE KERNEL COMMONS
"Others compile their own theories into kernels and submit them to the terminal. The 23 kernels become 230. The Axiomatic Core remains — but the theories it governs are no longer one architect's. The terminal becomes a commons for executable thought."

What changes

  • Kernel submission protocol (Rust -> WASM)
  • Peer review / verification pipeline
  • Contributor attribution in CAS manifest
  • LatentCollider operates on foreign kernels

Ars argument

  • Digital commons / open infrastructure
  • Scales beyond single author
  • Others' theories enter collision space
  • "Theory that cannot be compiled" becomes a shared standard
