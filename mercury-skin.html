Rayleigh body modes · the wobble
ωℓ²=ℓ(ℓ−1)(ℓ+2)σ/ρR³. ℓ=2 is prolate↔oblate — the drop's bounce. ℓ=3/ℓ=4 ride at exactly 1.936× and 3× and damp 2.8×/5.4× faster, so the fundamental outlives them. Mercury's viscosity is ~1.15e-7 m²/s: it rings for ages. Low ring-down = truthful.
