# THE PASSALUS KERNEL :: ARCHITECTURE OF DECAY

/// SYSTEM_OBJECTIVE
Synthesize structural integrity and generational survival out of depleted resources.

/// SYSTEM_CONSTRAINT
The environment provides zero readily bioavailable calories. The system must manufacture its own energy economy through mutualistic processing.

================================================================================
[ PARADOX AXIOMS ]
================================================================================

[■] 01 :: THE SCARCITY SYNTHESIS AXIOM
>> The Paradox: The primary food source is dead wood, which contains virtually no accessible nutrients. Despite this scarcity, the beetle builds a physically massive and socially complex colony.
>> The Architecture: Value is processed, not extracted. Adults rely on an "external rumen" – utilizing a gut microbiome and feces to pre-digest the wood. They then feed this processed material to their larvae. Raw capital is structurally useless until it is biologically processed and shared downwards.

[■] 02 :: THE PARASITIC DIVIDEND AXIOM
>> The Paradox: Beetles infected with the Chondronema passali nematode parasite do not degrade in performance. Instead, they consume 15% more wood, grow larger, and accelerate the ecological recycling of the forest.
>> The Architecture: Internal systemic friction does not lead to collapse. The system overcompensates for the parasitic load by increasing total metabolic throughput. This biological response ultimately enhances the system's service to the macro-environment.

[■] 03 :: THE CONFINEMENT STRENGTH AXIOM
>> The Paradox: A 1 to 2 gram Bess Beetle is capable of pulling up to 50 times its body weight. However, it is statistically 8 times stronger when pulling from inside the narrow confines of its self-excavated wood tunnel than when placed out in the open.
>> The Architecture: Absolute freedom degrades output. Maximum systemic force is only generated when the agent is strictly bounded by the physical constraints of its environment.

[■] 04 :: THE NON-REPRODUCTIVE LABOR AXIOM
>> The Paradox: The species lacks a formal reproductive caste system, yet young adults delay their own dispersal. They stay in the log, repair sibling pupal cases, and actively care for the next generation.
>> The Architecture: Subsocial survival outranks individual propagation. The highest expression of status within the system is the maintenance of the infrastructure rather than immediate expansion.

[■] 05 :: THE ACOUSTIC RESONANCE AXIOM
>> The Paradox: The beetles live in total darkness but possess the largest acoustic repertoire of any beetle, utilizing 14 distinct stridulation signals. When a distress signal is broadcast, the colony does not scatter; the beetles exhibit a "freeze" response.
>> The Architecture: In environments of total decay, high-fidelity signaling prevents panic. Data transmission enforces collective stillness rather than chaotic individual flight.
