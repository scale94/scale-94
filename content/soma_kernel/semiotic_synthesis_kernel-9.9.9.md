# semiotic_synthesis_kernel_9.9.9

The central thesis posits that meaning migrates through sound: one phoneme held fixed while its payload recompiles at every language border it crosses. The law of the street is the law of the kernel — every phonetic hop must also be a semantic hop, and both must point the same way. A hop that moves sound without meaning is cut at the border; the purity of the chain is enforced by what never enters the bag.
