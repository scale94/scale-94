## §0 — AXIOM OF VOLATILITY

**Core principle:** Intelligence that cannot volatilise cannot propagate.

## §1 — THE THREE ACCORDS

Perfumery organises scent into three temporal layers — top, heart, base. OCK maps these directly to computational signal propagation:

### §1.1 — Top Note ᛏ (Flash Signal)

- **Evaporation rate:** Immediate — 15 minutes
- **Computational analogue:** Interrupt handler. The first packet. The ping.
- **Thermodynamic state:** High vapour pressure, low molecular weight
- **Cognitive function:** Captures attention before deliberation begins
- **Failure mode:** Citrus-bright systems that spike and leave no trace — the tweet that trends and vanishes

Top notes are the SSH handshake of olfactory architecture. They establish connection. They are not the connection.

### §1.2 — Heart Note ᚺ (Carrier Signal)

- **Evaporation rate:** 20 minutes — 4 hours
- **Computational analogue:** Persistent process. The daemon. The loop that holds state.
- **Thermodynamic state:** Moderate vapour pressure, aromatic complexity
- **Cognitive function:** Sustains engagement past the initial interrupt
- **Failure mode:** Floral-sweet midrange that plateaus into white noise — the product that onboards but never retains

Heart notes are the runtime. They define what the system *does* between boot and shutdown.

### §1.3 — Base Note ᛒ (Residual Signal)

- **Evaporation rate:** 4 hours — days
- **Computational analogue:** Persistent storage. The write to disk. The memory that survives power-off.
- **Thermodynamic state:** Low vapour pressure, high molecular weight (musks, resins, woods)
- **Cognitive function:** Forms long-term associative memory — scent-triggered recall
- **Failure mode:** Systems so heavy they never volatilise — the monolith that cannot adapt

Base notes are the kernel itself. They outlast the session.

## §4 — SILLAGE AS SIGNAL PROPAGATION

**Sillage** (French: *wake*) — the degree to which a fragrance projects from the wearer into ambient space.

Sillage is not volume. It is *reach at low concentration*. A fragrance with high sillage communicates across distance without shouting. This is the olfactory equivalent of a well-designed protocol: maximum information transfer at minimum bandwidth.


## §5 — MACERATION AS ANNEALING

A perfume is not finished when blended. It is *macerated* — sealed and left in darkness for weeks to months. During maceration, volatile components undergo slow chemical reactions: ester formation, oxidation, molecular recombination. The accord *settles*.

This is simulated annealing at room temperature. The system explores its energy landscape without external forcing, finding lower-energy configurations through thermal fluctuation alone.

**Kernel application:** Any TRANSMUTE output benefits from a maceration phase. The kernel document that improves after sitting unread for a week has undergone maceration. The Rust crate that compiles cleaner after a refactor cycle has annealed. Urgency is the enemy of base notes.
