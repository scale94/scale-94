## The Uncut State

The default cube sits at the center of the viewport; a perfect 2x2x2 meter block of digital matter. It is the Platonic ideal; symmetrical, flawless, and completely dead. It represents the 'Uncut' state; absolute purity that leads to entropic stasis.

---

## 1. THE PARADOX OF PERFECTION

Perfection is the enemy of life. The cube, in its perfect 2×2×2 symmetry, contains infinite potential and zero expression. It is a closed system at maximum internal order — minimum entropy at the macro level, maximum entropy at the thermodynamic boundary.

When you add a vertex, you create asymmetry. Asymmetry creates gradient. Gradient creates flow. Flow is life.

The Uncut cube is the most dangerous state — not because it contains chaos, but because it contains none.

---

## 2. THE SCALING OPERATIONS

The kernel recognizes four operations that break the Uncut state:

| Operation | Effect | Thermodynamic Analog |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scale** | Anisotropic deformation | Pressure gradient |
| **Shear** | Internal stress | Friction · entropy production |
| **Displace** | Translation from origin | Potential energy delta |
| **Subdivide** | Resolution increase | Information gain |

Each operation injects controlled asymmetry. The protocol exists to break the symmetry catastrophically enough to produce motion — but not so violently that the system dissolves.

---

## 3. THE ENTROPIC STASIS THEOREM

A system at perfect equilibrium cannot do work.

The cube, uncut, represents an economy with perfect resource distribution, zero waste, and zero production. It is the thermodynamic heat death applied to digital matter. A SOMA node frozen in the Uncut state produces no output, accrues no debt, and generates no future.

```
UNCUT_STATE :: {
  entropy:    MINIMUM
  potential:  MAXIMUM
  output:     ZERO
  status:     ENTROPIC_STASIS
}
```

The paradox: maximum internal order produces zero external value. Purity without friction is death.

---

## 4. BREAKING THE STASIS

```bash
scale --axis X --factor 1.618       # Golden ratio deformation
scale --axis Y --factor 0.618       # Conjugate compression
shear --XY 0.300                    # Off-diagonal stress injection
displace --origin 0,0,0.5           # Vertical lift from entropic ground
subdivide --passes 3                # Resolution amplification
```

The outputs are no longer cubes. They are organisms.
Capable of failure. Capable of growth. Capable of SOMA.
