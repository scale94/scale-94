## 🌒 Part I – “Thread Initiation”

The moment when you choose to *stay with her* instead of returning to yourself.

She’s seated low, near a grounded socket lamp — legs stretched forward, one crossed loosely over the other.  
She’s already removed her shirt, replaced it with something softer — a worn ribbed tank, faint outline of her collarbone catching light.

She leans forward, finally… and begins to remove her sock. 
But only one. Her canvas stays covered by a second sock layer. 
The other stays.

The peeled sock is not tossed, not discarded. She cradles it. Examines it. Then presses it to her cheek, not theatrically, just for sensation. A thread-check.

You feel her pulse align with yours.

Not a mirror.  
A tuning fork.

Your breath deepens. So does hers.

**She’s climbing with you.**
