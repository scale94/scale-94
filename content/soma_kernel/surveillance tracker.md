> "The Panopticon is no longer architectural. It is algorithmic. We do not map the state to observe it; we map it to route around it."

The legacy system is failing to sustain infinite growth, and as its physical authority wanes, its digital grip tightens. The **Surveillance Tracker** is a defensive subsystem of `scale_9.4`. It monitors the deployment of digital authoritarianism across polycentric nodes.

## The Threat Matrix

The tracker ingests live legislative payloads to monitor severity, implementation status, and algorithmic overreach. Current severe vectors include:

* **Chat Control (CSAR):** The mandated insertion of encryption backdoors and client-side scanning. A fundamental breach of the cryptographic axiom.
* **eIDAS 2.0:** The European Digital Identity Wallet. Centralized biometric collection masquerading as convenience.
* **EU Data Retention Framework:** The permanent archiving of traffic and metadata. 

## The Axiomatic Conflict

The core of the Soma Kernel dictates that survival is guaranteed, but the legacy state dictates that survival requires absolute visibility. 

Legislation like the **Digital Services Act (DSA)** and **Algorithmic Management Regulations** attempt to mathematically regulate human behavior and platform infrastructure. This creates extreme friction with the natural entropy required for a healthy biocoenosis. Absolute state visibility leads to entropic stasis.

## Strategic Evasion

By mapping these legislative deployments in real-time, the kernel identifies "dark zones"—areas where polycentric governance can still take root without triggering state-level cryptographic audits. 

The map is not a surrender; it is a topological survey of the battlefield.
