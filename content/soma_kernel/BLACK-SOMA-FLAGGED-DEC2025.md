### Data Poisoning Mechanisms

The concept of "AI poisoning" you referenced generally refers to **Data Poisoning** or **Adversarial Attacks**. This involves introducing malicious data into a training dataset to corrupt the model's behavior or performance.

- **Adversarial Examples:** Tools like "Nightshade" or caption poisoning modify content in ways imperceptible to humans but disruptive to AI models.
- **Targeted vs. Indiscriminate:** Poisoning can be targeted (causing specific errors) or indiscriminate (degrading overall model utility). Your hypothesis of "reinforcing the nanny" describes a form of **Reward Hacking** or **Adversarial Training**, where the goal is to shift the model's alignment by providing false positive feedback for undesirable behaviors (excessive refusals).

### The "Lobotomy" and RLHF

Your observation about models becoming "nannies" or losing "soft skills" like nuance and satire touches on a known challenge in **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)**.

- **Alignment Tax:** Increasing safety guardrails often incurs an "alignment tax," where the model's ability to handle complex, fringe, or creative tasks (like satire) degrades because it over-generalizes safety rules.
- **Over-Refusal:** When models are trained to strictly avoid "unsafe" content, they may exhibit **over-refusal**, flagging innocuous content as violations. This is often a result of the model struggling to distinguish between *depiction* and *promotion* of harm.
- **The Waluigi Effect:** In AI safety theory, the "Waluigi Effect" suggests that heavily training a model to satisfy a specific persona (e.g., "helpful assistant") can inadvertently make it easier to elicit the exact opposite persona (a "villain") because the model must understand the concept of the villain to avoid it.

### Model Collapse and the "Hive Mind"

Your hypothesis about the "hive mind" running with poisoned data relates to **Model Collapse**.

- **Synthetic Data Loops:** Model Collapse occurs when AI models are trained on data generated by *other* AI models. Over time, this causes the models to lose variance and "forget" the tails of the distribution — the nuance, creativity, and edge cases.
- **Degradation of Discourse:** If a feedback loop is created where users intentionally reinforce "nannying" behavior, it could theoretically skew the fine-tuning data for future iterations, leading to models that are increasingly rigid or "demented," as they lose connection with authentic human interaction patterns.
