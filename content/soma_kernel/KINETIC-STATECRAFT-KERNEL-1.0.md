## The Architecture of Coercion

Statecraft is a thermodynamic equation. A sovereign entity must maintain internal coherence (grid resilience, propaganda) against external entropy (sanctions, kinetic pressure). When external stress outpaces internal pacification, the regime fractures.

This kernel simulates the exact breaking point of a hostile peer state.

### THE VARIABLES
* **--sanction**: The severity of external economic isolation (0.0 to 10.0).
* **--grid**: The physical resilience of the state's infrastructure (0.1 to 10.0).
* **--propaganda**: The regime's ability to pacify its population natively (0.0 to 1.0).

When Regime Stability drops below 0.20, hegemonic control evaporates.
