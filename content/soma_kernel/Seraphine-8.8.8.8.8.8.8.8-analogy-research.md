## Abstract

We apply a 16-dimensional thermodynamic feature space to quantify structural similarity across three proposed cross-domain analogies. Each analogy pairs systems from different ontological domains; the feature vectors are derived from primary literature rather than narrative intuition. Raw cosine similarities before any rotational alignment range from 0.611 to 0.863. The results reveal that the strongest mathematical bridge exists between a condensed matter physics phenomenon (twisted bilayer graphene superconductivity at 1.1°) and a cognitive neuroscience state (flow), while the most compelling conceptual metaphor (Bouligand rotation ↔ polymorphic encryption) has the weakest mathematical grounding. This divergence between narrative compellingness and geometric similarity is itself the primary scientific finding.

## 4. Scientific Implications for Associative Reasoning

### 4.1 The Narrative-Geometry Divergence

The three pairs were proposed on the basis of conceptual narrative. Their computed similarities reveal:

- **Pair 2** (Bouligand ↔ PQC, cos 0.611): Most compelling narrative, weakest geometry. The analogy works as a teaching device but fails as a theoretical isomorphism. The divergence on synchrony (0.75), stochastic (0.75), game_theory (0.80), and information (0.70) indicates that the biological and cryptographic defense mechanisms are implemented in nearly orthogonal mathematical regimes.

- **Pair 3** (Magic angle ↔ Flow, cos 0.863): Weakest narrative (these systems seem maximally different), strongest geometry. The similarity is real and not easily discoverable by conceptual intuition alone. This is exactly the type of cross-domain bridge that a high-dimensional feature space is designed to find.

**The scientific claim:** Narrative compellingness and geometric similarity are negatively correlated in this sample. The analogies we find most intuitively satisfying (Bouligand ↔ polymorphic defense) turn out to be the least mathematically grounded. The surprising connections (quantum phase transition ↔ cognitive flow) that resist narrative packaging have the strongest structural support.

This is consistent with the broader analogical reasoning literature: Gentner's Structure-Mapping Theory (1983) predicts that the deepest analogies are between relational structures, not surface features. Hofstadter (1995) argues that the best analogies are those that survive the most rigorous attempts to break them. Pair 3 survives better than Pair 2.

### 4.2 What the 16D Space Reveals That Narrative Analysis Cannot

The dimension that most disrupts intuitive analogy assessment is **temporal [dim 7]**.

The Arapaima scale (white_irid: 0.80) and the Bouligand mechanism (bouligand_36: 0.10) are from the same organism, yet score divergently on temporal depth. This is not an error — it captures a genuine distinction: the *evolved material* encodes deep time; the *mechanical mechanism* operates instantaneously. The 16D space distinguishes these cleanly.

This distinction matters for bio-inspired design: copying the geometric mechanism (36° rotation) is straightforward; recapitulating the evolutionary optimization that selected that exact angle is not.

### 4.3 The Saponification Prediction

The bone fusion algorithm predicts that Pair 2 (cos 0.611) will require the maximum number of saponification iterations to reach the convergence threshold (cos > 0.9990). The algorithm will strip metabolic_cost [dim 14] and soften modularity [dim 15] to force convergence — but the fundamental divergences on synchrony, stochastic, game_theory, and information are not stripped by saponification (these are not metabolic dimensions).

**Testable prediction:** When `run bone fusion --nodes 31 --threshold 0.9990` is executed, the WHITE_IRID ↔ PITCH_BLACK_STEEL pair will fuse first (raw cos 0.855), MAGIC_ANGLE_1P1 ↔ ZERO_EFFORT_FLOW second (raw cos 0.863, but higher dimensionality makes it marginally harder), and BOULIGAND_36 ↔ POLYMORPH_PQC last — possibly triggering FusionRejected if the modularity barrier cannot be dissolved within MAX_SAPONIFICATION = 32 iterations.
