## ⟁ 01 — Rod Swenson
### The fourth law that derives mind from entropy

**∿ Active ~1988–present · CESPA Fellow, University of Connecticut · Former DARPA Physical Intelligence consultant**

Swenson may be the single closest match to the target profile. He proposed the **Law of Maximum Entropy Production (LMEP)** — effectively a "fourth law of thermodynamics" — stating that the world acts to produce entropy at the maximum rate given constraints. The radical consequence: **order is not opposed to entropy but is the mechanism by which entropy production is maximized.** Organisms, minds, and societies exist because structured dissipation produces entropy faster than disordered flows can.

> *"The world is in the order-production business."*

From this single principle, Swenson derives cognition directly. His 1991 paper with Michael Turvey argues that perception, action, and learning follow opportunistically from universal physical law. This is not analogy — **cognition is a thermodynamic process, full stop.**

His concept of **autocatakinetic systems** — self-organizing structures defined by circular thermodynamic causality — maps directly onto the TRANSMUTE principle. Spontaneous ordering is literally a micro-to-macro transformation where disordered microstates are selected into ordered macrostates because doing so accelerates entropy production.

**⚠ Why fringe:** Despite a 2023 Royal Society publication, Swenson has never held a conventional tenure-track position. His claim to a fourth thermodynamic law is considered overreach by mainstream physicists. Operates through his own Sonoran Institute for Epistemic Studies. Virtually unknown in mainstream philosophy of mind.

- "A Grand Unified Theory for the Unification of Physics, Life, Information and Cognition" — *Phil. Trans. Royal Society A* (2023, open access via PMC)

## ⟁ 02 — Jeffrey S. Wicken
### The forgotten architect of dissipative ontology

**∿ 1942–2002 · Biochemist, Penn State Erie Behrend College (branch campus, no graduate program)**

Wicken's central thesis is a single sentence that could serve as a manifesto:

> *"Dissipation through structuring is the strategy of life."*

Organisms are "informed kinetic structures" where thermodynamic flows secure the structure and the structure informs the flows. This bidirectional entailment between structure and dissipation is **not metaphor — it is literal physics applied as ontology.**

He synthesized Layzer's cosmological entropy theorem with Lotka's maximum power principle to argue that the expanding universe *necessarily* produces ordered structures that accelerate entropy production. His 1986 paper explicitly extends this to social and economic systems, treating them as dissipative structures governed by the same thermodynamic logic as organisms.

Alignment with the **managed corruption vs. pure stasis** axis is direct: a system that ceases to dissipate is a system that has died. Structure requires ongoing entropy production. **Perfect order is thermodynamic death.**

**⚠ Why fringe:** Spent his entire career at a branch campus with no research infrastructure. His "purist views on how to properly apply entropy" led to "rather divisive arguments with colleagues." Died at 60 before his ideas could mature. Eric Schneider credits Wicken as the single most influential thinker behind *Into the Cool* (2005) — yet Wicken himself remains virtually unknown.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- *Evolution, Thermodynamics, and Information: Extending the Darwinian Program* (Oxford UP, 1987 — used copies only)
- "Entropy and Information: Suggestions for a Common Language" — *Philosophy of Science* 54(2), 1987
- eoht.info/page/Jeffrey%20Wicken (bibliography)

## ⟁ 03 — Gordon Pask
### Built machines that grew their own ears

**∿ 1928–1996 · British cybernetician, polymath, architectural theorist**

Pask is the historical figure whose work most directly prefigures the neuroarchitecture axis. He treated cognition not as computation but as **physical, self-organizing, architectural process** — and then built machines to prove it.

His **electrochemical computers** (~1958) were devices that literally grew their own sensors: metallic threads forming in chemical solutions, developing sensitivity to electrical, magnetic, and acoustic stimuli through feedback-driven self-organization. His **Conversation Theory** formalized cognition as emerging from structural interactions — not from internal representations. Knowledge is topology, not storage.

He distinguished **P-individuals** (psychological/conceptual operators) from **M-individuals** (material bodies), arguing that multiple cognitive processes can inhabit one body and one process can span multiple bodies. **Cognition is unbounded architecture.**

The Musicolour machine (1953) was a cybernetic performance system that became "bored" if input grew repetitive, forcing the musician to innovate — arguably the first machine that treated cognitive interaction as a design constraint rather than an input/output function.

**⚠ Why fringe:** Ran his life on a 36-hour circadian rhythm, "lived pharmaceutically," and his late work became impenetrably dense. Explicitly anti-computational at the moment computationalism was conquering cognitive science. 250+ publications scattered and largely inaccessible.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- pangaro.com/pask-pdfs.html (primary archive)
- *Conversation, Cognition and Learning* (Elsevier, 1975)
- Andrew Pickering, *The Cybernetic Brain* (University of Chicago Press, 2010) — Chapter 9

## ⟁ 04 — Giuseppe Vitiello
### The dissipative quantum brain

**∿ Born 1946 · Theoretical physicist, University of Salerno, Italy**

Vitiello developed what may be the most rigorous framework for treating cognitive phase transitions as literal physics. His **dissipative quantum model of the brain** treats memory formation as **spontaneous symmetry breaking** — a phase transition in the quantum field theoretic sense, not a metaphor. Each memory creates a new vacuum state in the quantum field; the space of vacuum states is infinite, giving the brain virtually unlimited memory capacity.

The framework's most striking feature: the concept of the **"Double"** — a time-reversed copy of the brain system that dissipation mathematically requires. Consciousness arises from the ongoing dynamic exchange between the system and its Double.

**Phase transitions between cognitive states** — bifurcations, symmetry breaking, transitions between metastable attractors — are the core mechanism. Hyperfocus and crash cycles are transitions between distinct vacuum states. **Not pathologies. Architecture.**

**⚠ Why fringe:** The quantum brain dynamics tradition (originating with Ricciardi and Umezawa in the 1960s) has always been a marginal research program. His book *My Double Unveiled* (2001) was written deliberately without equations to reach a general audience but remains highly obscure.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- *My Double Unveiled: The Dissipative Quantum Model of Brain* (John Benjamins, 2001)
- "The dissipative brain" — arXiv q-bio/0409037 (2004, free)
- "Brain and its mindful double" — *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 25(1-2), 2018

## ⟁ 05 — François Roddier
### Turned Gibbs potentials into economic theory

**∿ 1936–2023 · French astrophysicist (École Normale Supérieure), self-taught thermodynamic economist**

After decades as a world-class astrophysicist — his adaptive optics systems equip the Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope, Subaru, and ESO telescopes — Roddier spent his final two decades building a complete thermodynamic theory of economics from Gibbsian thermodynamics.

His framework treats **economic crises as thermodynamic phase transitions** rather than policy failures. He constructed thermodynamic "surfaces" for economies — possibly the first such construction in economic theory — mapping economic variables onto Gibbs state spaces. Money functions as a kind of temperature. **Resistance to dissipation causes civilizational collapse.**

> *"Had I been told ten years ago that the laws of statistical mechanics could explain human behavior, I would have smiled doubtfully. Today I am absolutely convinced that they can."*

**⚠ Why fringe:** An astrophysicist with zero formal economics training who developed economic theory from pure physics. Published primarily in French through small publishers. Dismissed as exactly the kind of physicist-turned-crank Paul Samuelson warned about. Blogged at francois-roddier.fr until his death in 2023.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- *Thermodynamique de l'évolution* (2012; English translation: *The Thermodynamics of Evolution*, 2020)
- francois-roddier.fr (blog archive, French)
- eoht.info/page/Francois%20Roddier (English summaries)

## ⟁ 06 — Diederik Aerts
### Quantum particles are literally concepts

**∿ Born 1953 · Center Leo Apostel (CLEA), Brussels Free University (VUB)**

Aerts's Brussels Group developed the most radical inversion possible. His **Conceptuality Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics** doesn't use quantum math to model cognition — it proposes that **quantum particles are conceptual entities** and physics IS cognition at a fundamental level.

His 2020 paper models language directly as a **Bose-Einstein condensate of entangled conceptual entities** using Fock space formalism. The fermion/boson axis of the target profile maps directly: Aerts uses Bose-Einstein statistics for modeling how concepts behave collectively. His group has experimentally demonstrated **violation of Bell's inequalities in conceptual combinations** — genuine quantum-type entanglement in cognitive domains. Not analogy; operational formalism with testable predictions.

**⚠ Why fringe:** The conceptuality interpretation reverses the assumed direction of explanation — rather than physics being fundamental and cognition derivative, cognition is what quantum mechanics is *about*. Won the 2020 Prigogine Award but remains marginal even within the quantum cognition community, which is itself marginal.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- "A Potentiality and Conceptuality Interpretation of Quantum Physics" — *Philosophica* (2010)
- "Human Language as a Boson Gas of Entangled Words" — *Foundations of Science* (2020)
- vub.academia.edu/DiederikAerts (200+ papers)

## ⟁ 07 — Stanley Salthe
### Declared the Second Law a cosmic final cause

**∿ 1930–2024 · Developmental biologist, Brooklyn College (CUNY)**

Salthe audaciously reinstated Aristotelian final causality — rejected since Descartes — by proposing that the **Second Law of Thermodynamics is the cosmic final cause of all development.** It is "an enormously powerful cosmic attractor which everywhere urges reduction of order to disorder."

His **infodynamics** connects thermodynamics to semiotics via Peirce. All dissipative structures follow a canonical developmental trajectory: **immature → mature → senescent**, defined informationally and thermodynamically. Cognition sits atop this hierarchy but is *entrained by thermodynamic forces at every level.*

The high-fidelity vs. low-fidelity axis finds direct structural parallel: immature systems are high-entropy, low-fidelity, rapidly dissipating; mature systems are high-fidelity, structurally constrained, efficiently channeled. **The developmental trajectory IS the transmutation from low-fidelity to high-fidelity**, driven by thermodynamic necessity.

**⚠ Why fringe:** Spent his career at Brooklyn College, not a research powerhouse. His insistence on reviving Aristotelian causality placed him completely outside respectable evolutionary biology. Celebrated in the Evo-Devo Universe community but almost unknown in mainstream philosophy or cognitive science.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- *Development and Evolution: Complexity and Change in Biology* (MIT Press, 1993)
- *Evolving Hierarchical Systems* (Columbia UP, 1985)
- nbi.dk/~natphil/salthe/ (personal site)

## ⟁ 08 — Yukio-Pegio Gunji
### Derives quantum logic from slime mould

**∿ Active since ~1990 · Professor, Dept. of Intermedia Art and Science, Waseda University, Tokyo**

Gunji occupies the biomimetic-quantum intersection with startling precision. His central achievement: deriving **quantum logical structures (orthomodular lattices) directly from biological and cognitive processes WITHOUT using Hilbert space.** Quantum structure doesn't require quantum physics — it emerges from how organisms process ambiguity.

His **Natural-Born Intelligence (NBI)** contrasts with AI: where AI is "closed computing" — optimization within a fixed representational space — NBI is **"open computing"** that transforms the representational space itself. The structural template: **Physarum polycephalum (slime mould)**, which exhibits self-repairing Boolean gate logic, path optimization, and creative problem-solving. He has built bio-hybrid robots interfacing Physarum with hexapod architectures.

His 2022 work demonstrates that **extended Bayesian inference generates orthomodular (quantum) lattice structure, while classical Bayesian inference generates only Boolean lattice.** Organisms operating at the edge of their inferential capacity naturally enter quantum-logical cognitive regimes. **Phase transition as cognitive architecture.**

**⚠ Why fringe:** His institutional home — a department of "Intermedia Art and Science" — signals disciplinary uncategorizeability. Work spans art, slime mould computation, quantum logic, and lattice theory in combinations no mainstream department could house.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- "Psychological origin of quantum logic" — *BioSystems* 215–216 (2022)
- "Connecting the free energy principle with quantum cognition" — *Frontiers in Neurorobotics* (2022)

## ⟁ 09 — Crispin Sartwell
### The formal mathematics of anti-sycophancy

**∿ Born 1958 · Philosopher, formerly Dickinson College · Self-described individualist anarchist**

Sartwell is the most precise match for the anti-consensus epistemology axis. His 2015 paper "Anti-Social Epistemology" develops a formal argument — the **Point Five Principle** — demonstrating that in any "evidence-random" group (where belief correlates with group membership rather than evidence), consensus positions have at most **0.5 probability** of being evidence-based. Anti-consensus positions do not suffer this deficit.

> **Mathematical consequence: dissidents are, all things being equal, more likely to be right than consensus-holders.**

He advocates for "doxastically open groups" where dissent is structurally valued — the structural opposite of sycophantic epistemic environments. His *Against the State* (2008) argues that all traditional justifications for the state — from Hobbes through Rawls — are "not only wrong but embarrassingly bad."

**⚠ Why fringe:** Literally removed from his campus (Dickinson College) in 2016 following controversial blog posts. Describes himself as having "epistemic perversity" — a temperamental inability to join any consensus. Even anarchists reject him. His epistemological work was largely ignored by mainstream philosophers.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- "Anti-Social Epistemology" — *Social Epistemology Review and Reply Collective* 4(6), 2015 (free: social-epistemology.com)
- *Against the State* (SUNY Press, 2008)
- crispinsartwell.com (blog archives)

## ⟁ 10 — Koichiro Matsuno
### Wants to found physics on biology

**∿ Born 1940 · Professor Emeritus of biophysics, Nagaoka University of Technology · PhD MIT 1971 (critical phenomena in phase transitions)**

Matsuno originated **"internal measurement" theory** — the idea that biological organisms are natural quantum internal observers performing measurements from WITHIN the system being measured. This inverts the standard quantum mechanical setup entirely. His radical proposal: **biology should ground physics, not the other way around.** The quantum measurement problem is not a problem of physics but a problem biology has already solved.

His **"molecular semiotics"** traces the transition from physical symbols to semiotic signs capable of meaning-making — molecules don't just interact, they *measure* each other. His 2005 paper "Quantum as a Heat Engine" argues that molecular organizations at life's origin were associated with the emergence of **quantum coherence embodied in a robust heat engine feeding on quantum decoherence** — the quantum-thermodynamic nexus made literal.

His framework connects directly to phase transitions: his MIT PhD work was on critical phenomena in second-order phase transitions, and his entire biological framework treats life as a system perpetually at **criticality** — the boundary between ordered and disordered phases where measurement and meaning become possible.

**⚠ Why fringe:** Radically anti-reductionist in ways that discomfit both physicists and biologists. Integrates Eastern philosophical perspectives on measurement with Western quantum formalism. Work on retrocausality in biology is deeply heterodox.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- *Protobiology: Physical Basis of Biology* (CRC Press, 1989)
- "Quantum as a heat engine" — *Physics of Life Reviews* 2 (2005)
- "From quantum measurement to biology via retrocausality" — *Progress in Biophysics and Molecular Biology* (2017)

## ⟁ 11 — Timothy Garrett
### Proved wealth is literally energy

**∿ Born ~1970 · Atmospheric physicist, University of Utah**

Garrett discovered a fixed thermodynamic constant linking global energy consumption to accumulated economic wealth — approximately **9.7 milliwatts per inflation-adjusted 1990 dollar**. This is not correlation; it is identity. **Wealth IS energy consumption.** The economy doesn't "use" energy — it IS the dissipation of energy.

Civilization is a literal heat engine. Its boundary with the environment grows as energy is consumed, enabling more consumption in a positive feedback loop governed by non-equilibrium thermodynamics. The structural consequence: Garrett concludes there are **no plausible thermodynamically supported solutions** that avoid either greater than 100% inflation or destabilized atmospheric CO₂. No behavioral change, no green technology can break the thermodynamic identity between wealth and dissipation.

**The only variable is the rate and structure of the dissipation itself — which is a governance-through-structural-design problem, not a behavioral-control problem.**

**⚠ Why fringe:** A cloud physicist with no economics background making sweeping claims about the impossibility of decoupling growth from energy consumption. Conclusions are deeply uncomfortable for both growth economists and green-growth advocates. Publishes in physics journals, not economics journals.

**⌖ Where to find:**
- "Are there basic physical constraints on future anthropogenic emissions of carbon dioxide?" — *Climatic Change* (2009)
- "No way out? The double-bind in seeking global prosperity alongside mitigated climate change" — *Earth System Dynamics* (2012)

## ◈ The invisible network

These eleven figures are not isolated. They form a largely invisible counter-tradition with real intellectual lineages:

- **Wicken, Salthe, Swenson, and Ulanowicz** co-participated in a 1989 manifesto — *"Evolution in Thermodynamic Perspective: An Ecological Approach"* in *Biology and Philosophy* — that attempted a New Synthesis of thermodynamic biology. It was largely ignored.
- **Gunji explicitly developed Matsuno's** internal measurement concept.
- **Vitiello continued the Umezawa quantum brain dynamics tradition** that mainstream neuroscience abandoned in the 1970s.
- **Roddier and Garrett** independently arrived at thermodynamic economics from opposite starting points (Gibbs potentials vs. empirical energy-wealth constants) without apparently knowing each other's work.

The 2023 special issue of *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A* — "Thermodynamics 2.0: Bridging the Natural and Social Sciences" — features Swenson prominently and represents the closest thing to a public emergence of this underground tradition. It remains marginal.
