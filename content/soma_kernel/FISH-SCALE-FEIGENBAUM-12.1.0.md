However, the Bouligand structure does not require full phyllotactic divergence. It requires *interlocking overlap*.

The Fish Scale offset is the sub-harmonic:

$$\theta_{FSK} = \frac{\phi_g}{\lfloor \varphi^2 \rceil} \approx \frac{137.5°}{4} \approx 34.4°$$
