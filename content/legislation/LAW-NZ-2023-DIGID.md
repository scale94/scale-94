# Digital Identity Services Trust Framework

## Digital Identity Services Trust Framework Act 2023

## Description

Establishes voluntary accreditation scheme for digital identity service providers. Creates Trust Framework Authority and Trust Framework Board to oversee standards for privacy, security, and data sharing. While currently voluntary, establishes infrastructure for potential future mandatory digital ID requirements.

## Severity Rationale

> Voluntary accreditation system [+0], no criminal penalties for users [+0], biometric data collection by accredited providers [+0.5], potential chilling effect if widely adopted [+0.5], mission creep to employment and services possible [+0.5], some judicial oversight through Privacy Commissioner [+0], digital exclusion risk for those without technology access [+0.5]. Total: 3/5 (Medium)

## Implementation Notes

Came into effect 1 July 2024. Trust Framework Rules released November 2024. Accreditation is voluntary but creates pathway to future mandatory requirements. Includes Māori Advisory Group for te ao Māori perspectives on identity. Rules cover consent, privacy impact assessments, security, and data management. Critics note it does little to address bigger issues of digital rights and online identity data.
