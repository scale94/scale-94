# Digital ID Act 2024 (MyGovID Expansion)

## Description

Legislation expanding myGovID from 130 government services to private sector (banks, telcos, utilities) by 2026, with biometric facial verification and 'Trust Exchange' enabling cross-sector data sharing.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application to government services, expanding to private sector [+1], administrative penalties for non-compliance by providers [+0.5], biometric collection (facial verification) [+1], chilling effect on organizing if required for employment [+0.5], explicit mission creep to private sector banking/telco by 2026-2027 [+1], oversight by ACCC and Services Australia (not judicial) [+0.5], digital exclusion for those without smartphones/bank accounts [+1]. Total: 5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Commenced December 2024; Phase 3 (private sector) allows banks, telcos, utilities to use myGovID by 2026-2027. Phase 4 allows private sector IDs for government services. Claims 'voluntary' but alternative pathways being systematically defunded. 'Trust Exchange' (TEx) enables credential sharing across government wallets. Senate amendments tightened liability but expanded ACCC oversight rather than judicial. Biometric data stored by accredited providers (including private sector) under TDIF standards. Union concerns about employer coercion to use for employment verification despite 'voluntary' claims.
