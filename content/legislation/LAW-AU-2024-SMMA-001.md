# Social Media Minimum Age Ban (Under-16s)

## Online Safety Amendment (Social Media Minimum Age) Act 2024

| Field | Value |

| **Jurisdiction** | National |

## Description

World-first ban on social media accounts for under-16s effective December 10, 2025, requiring platforms to implement 'reasonable steps' age verification including biometric estimation or government ID submission.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal age verification required for all social media users [+1], criminal penalties for platforms ($49.5M AUD fines) [+1], biometric age estimation or government ID collection [+1], severe chilling effect on youth organizing and political expression [+1], mission creep to other platforms explicit in legislation [+1], eSafety Commissioner discretion (not judicial) [+0.5], digital exclusion of under-16s and those without ID [+1]. Total: 5.5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Active enforcement from December 10, 2025. Platforms mass-deleting under-16 accounts. Exemptions for messaging apps, gaming, and educational services (subject to change). Penalties up to $49.5M AUD for non-compliance. 'Reasonable steps' includes facial age estimation creating biometric databases of all users (not just under-16s). No penalties for children/parents but creates surveillance infrastructure. Digital Rights Watch and EFA opposed as 'treating internet as cigarettes.' Sets global precedent being watched by UK, US states. Creates 'digital exodus' of youth to unregulated platforms. Union concerns about inability to organize young workers via social media.
