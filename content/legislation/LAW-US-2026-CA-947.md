# No Robo Bosses Act (California)

## Description

California legislation establishing worker protections against algorithmic management by prohibiting solely automated firing/discipline decisions, banning predictive behavior analysis, and requiring human oversight with transparency for ADS use in employment.

## Severity Rationale

> Applies to employers using ADS, not universal population [+0], no surveillance created but restricts existing [+0], prevents irreversible algorithmic discrimination [+0], explicitly PROTECTS worker organizing by preventing predictive retaliation [+0], prevents mission creep of worker surveillance [+0], establishes judicial oversight via private right of action [+0], reduces digital exclusion by requiring human review [+0]. Total: 0/7 → 2/5 (Low severity as protective legislation, included to show counter-surveillance efforts)

## Implementation Notes

Reintroduced February 2026 after Governor Newsom vetoed SB 7 in October 2025. Represents legislative resistance to worker surveillance. Key protections: prohibits 'predictive behavior analysis' that profiles workers for organizing activity, requires human investigation before discipline/termination, mandates post-use notice and data access rights. California Federation of Labor Unions (AFL-CIO) sponsoring. Creates private right of action for workers. Directly counteracts Amazon-style algorithmic firing and 'robo-rating' systems in gig economy. If enacted, would be strongest state-level protection against algorithmic management in US. Faces federal preemption risk under Trump administration threats to state AI regulation.
