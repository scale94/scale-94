# Terrorism Suppression Act Amendments (Proposed)

## Description

Proposed amendments to terrorism laws that would criminalize 'expressing support' for designated organizations and expand surveillance powers. Critics argue changes could be used against protest movements and political organizations, following similar UK legislation. Accompanied by proposals to give police unilateral power to ban protests.

## Severity Rationale

> Broad application to protest movements and organizations [+1], criminal penalties for membership and 'support' [+1], surveillance powers expanded [+0.5], explicit chilling effect on protests and political organizing [+1], mission creep from terrorism to protest movements [+1], limited judicial oversight for designations [+0.5], potential digital exclusion through surveillance [+0]. Total: 4/5 (High)

## Implementation Notes

Proposed in context of Five Eyes intelligence sharing and 'war on terror' expansion. NZCCL Chair Thomas Beagle warned: 'People will be criminalized not just for being members of an organisation but for expressing support for the issue it was focused on.' Linked to Independent Police Conduct Authority proposal to give police power to unilaterally ban protests. Part of broader pattern across Five Eyes (US, UK, Australia, Canada, NZ) of expanding internal security powers.
