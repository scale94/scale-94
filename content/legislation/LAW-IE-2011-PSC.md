# Public Services Card (Biometric ID Database)

## Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005 (as amended) - Public Services Card provisions

## Description

National ID card with biometric facial recognition templates required for accessing public services including welfare payments, driving license renewals, and National Childcare Scheme. State denied biometric processing but Data Protection Commission ruled otherwise in June 2025.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application (millions of citizens for essential services) [+1], irreversible biometric collection (facial templates) [+1], digital exclusion (required for welfare, childcare, driving licenses) [+1], no judicial oversight for data access [+1], mission creep from welfare to multiple services [+1]. No criminal penalties directly. Total: 5/5 but reduced to 4 due to ongoing challenge.

## Implementation Notes

LONG-RUNNING CIVIL LIBERTIES BATTLE. Key events: (1) 2021: DPC investigation began after Digital Rights Ireland complaint; (2) June 2025: DPC ruled PSC uses biometric data, fined State EUR 550,000, ordered discontinuation within 9 months unless lawful basis found; (3) State appealed July 2025 - appeal ongoing; (4) 24 outstanding Circuit Court cases by citizens; (5) Previous battle over mandatory nature ended in 2021 settlement. Current status: Card still widely used while appeal pending. Concerns: (1) State denied biometric processing for years claiming 'arithmetic template' not biometric - DPC rejected this; (2) Function creep from welfare to driving licenses, childcare, other services; (3) No clear legal basis for biometric processing per DPC; (4) Millions of citizens' facial templates in database. Opposition: Digital Rights Ireland leading legal challenge. 24 individual court cases pending. Workers' concern: Required for accessing employment-related services (childcare support), creates surveillance infrastructure that could be used against undocumented workers.
