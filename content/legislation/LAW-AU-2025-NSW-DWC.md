# NSW Digital White Card (Construction Worker ID)

## Service NSW Digital White Card Program (Work Health and Safety Regulation 2017 amendments)

## Description

Mandatory digitization of construction industry safety credentials requiring workers to use Service NSW app with MyServiceNSW Account, creating biometric-linked digital trails for site access while employers verify via QR code scanning.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal for construction workers (~600,000 in NSW) [+0.5], administrative penalties for non-compliance [+0.5], permanent digital record via Service NSW app [+0.5], potential chilling effect on undocumented construction workers [+0.5], mission creep to other trade licenses explicit [+1], no judicial oversight for data access [+0.5], digital exclusion requires smartphone/MyServiceNSW Account [+1]. Total: 4/7 → 3/5

## Implementation Notes

Active since February 2021 but expanded 2025-2026 as 'first Digital Trade Licence' with digitization of all trade licenses planned. Construction workers (laborers, tradespeople, supervisors) must maintain smartphone with charged battery and readable screen or face exclusion from sites. QR code verification allows employers to track site entry/exit. Linked to MyServiceNSW Account which requires 100 points of ID and biometric verification. Creates two-tier system: digital card holders vs. those unable/unwilling to use smartphones. Physical card still 'mandatory backup' but digital preferred. Terms require acceptance of broad data sharing. Connected to broader 'Digital White Card' national conversation but NSW first jurisdiction. Construction unions (CFMEU) concerned about employer surveillance via verification tracking but also recognize safety compliance benefits. Undocumented workers face barriers as digital system more traceable than physical cards.
