# Signals Intelligence Act (FRA Law) Amendments

## Lag (2008:717) om signalspaning i försvarsunderrättelseverksamhet (amended 2024)

## Description

Governs FRA (Försvarets Radioanstalt) bulk interception of all cable-based communications crossing Swedish borders. 2024 amendments expanded mandate to include 'strategic mapping of foreign direct investments' and introduced exception for domestic communications collection in times of war. ECHR judgment found oversight ineffective.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application - all cable communications passing Swedish borders [+1], criminal penalties for unauthorized disclosure [+0.5], mass data collection and storage [+1], severe chilling effect on journalist-source and lawyer-client communications [+1], mission creep to strategic foreign investment mapping [+1], ineffective oversight - Inspectorate cannot bind FRA to cease unlawful surveillance [+1], digital exclusion not applicable but mass surveillance [+0]. Total: 5/5 (Extreme)

## Implementation Notes

ECtHR Grand Chamber judgment May 25, 2021 found Swedish signals intelligence regime violated Article 8 ECHR due to ineffective oversight. 2024 amendments failed to address core issue: Foreign Intelligence Inspectorate cannot render legally binding decisions that unlawful surveillance must cease. Inspectorate can only act if FRA acts 'outside bounds of permit' - but permits set broad framework allowing compliance while breaching Convention. Centrum för rättvisa (Centre for Justice) continues litigation. SOU 2024:59 proposed further expansion including wartime domestic surveillance. FRA operates under Ministry of Defence. 16 years of bulk interception without effective oversight.
