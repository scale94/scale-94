# Olympics Surveillance Law / Loi JO2024

## Loi n° 2023-380 relative aux jeux Olympiques et Paralympiques de 2024

## Description

Legalized experimental AI-powered video surveillance for 2024 Paris Olympics, including algorithmic crowd analysis and biometric detection of 'suspicious behavior.' Law was temporary but established precedent and infrastructure for permanent deployment. Expanded police powers to use drones and facial recognition.

## Severity Rationale

> Applied to Olympic venues and transport but set precedent [+0.5], criminal penalties for security breaches [+0.5], AI-powered video surveillance and biometric crowd analysis [+1], chilling effect on protests near Olympic sites [+1], mission creep to permanent surveillance infrastructure [+1], ministerial discretion for deployment [+0.5], digital exclusion through algorithmic bias [+0.5]. Total: 4/5 (High)

## Implementation Notes

Temporary law for Olympic period but established permanent surveillance infrastructure. AI video analysis including crowd density detection, abandoned object detection, and behavior analysis. CNIL raised concerns about proportionality. La Quadrature du Net challenged facial recognition use before CNIL. Law expanded to allow police drone surveillance. Post-Olympics, infrastructure remains and has been used for other events. Sets dangerous precedent for 'temporary' surveillance becoming permanent.
