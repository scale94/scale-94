# Online Safety Act (Age Verification and Encryption)

## Online Safety Act 2023 (as amended)

## Description

Requires 'highly effective' age verification for adult content platforms by July 2025, with provisions enabling Ofcom to mandate client-side scanning of encrypted messages, effectively outlawing end-to-end encryption while creating mandatory identity verification for internet access.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application to all internet users [+1], criminal penalties for platforms (10% global revenue) [+1], irreversible biometric age estimation data collection [+1], chilling effect on organizing by requiring identity for social media [+1], mission creep from pornography to all 'harmful content' [+1], no judicial oversight for Ofcom notices [+1], digital exclusion for those without ID [+1]. Total: 7/7 → 5/5

## Implementation Notes

Active enforcement from July 25, 2025. Forces platforms to implement facial age estimation, government ID submission, or digital identity wallets. Section 121 grants Ofcom power to require 'accredited technology' (client-side scanning) for encrypted messaging. WhatsApp, Signal, Element threatened UK withdrawal. Apple removed Advanced Data Protection from UK market rather than comply with backdoor demands. Worker organizing communications vulnerable to employer-mandated surveillance under 'duties to prevent harmful content.'
