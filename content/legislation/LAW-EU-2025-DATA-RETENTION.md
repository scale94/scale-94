# EU Data Retention Framework (Proposed Update)

## Description

Proposed update to EU data retention rules following CJEU rulings. Would reintroduce mass telecommunications metadata retention for law enforcement access, with impact assessment expected Q1 2026.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application (all telecommunications users) [+1], no judicial oversight for retention (ministerial order) [+1], irreversible data collection (metadata databases) [+1], mission creep from 'serious crime' to 'national security' [+1], digital exclusion not applicable [0], criminal penalties for providers [+0.5]. Total: 4.5/5 rounded to 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Follows series of CJEU rulings (Digital Rights Ireland, Tele2 Sverige, La Quadrature du Net I & II) that progressively expanded member state leeway for data retention. Current proposal: (1) Impact assessment 2025; (2) Legislative proposal expected Q1 2026. Key concerns: (1) 'Lawful access by design' requirement - surveillance built into infrastructure; (2) Close coordination between state and industry for surveillance purposes; (3) CJEU allowed 'targeted retention' and IP address retention that member states have exploited. Belgium uses 'targeted retention' covering entire territory. France uses rotating short-term retention claiming continuous national security threat. Opposition: Digital rights groups argue data retention is 'stone dead' after Tele2 but being resurrected through exceptions. Workers' concern: Mass surveillance of communications chills union organizing and worker solidarity networks.
