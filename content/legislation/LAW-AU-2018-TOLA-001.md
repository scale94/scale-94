# Assistance and Access Act (TOLA)

## Telecommunications and Other Legislation Amendment (Assistance and Access) Act 2018

| Field | Value |

## Description

Active legislation compelling telecommunications providers and tech companies to provide technical assistance to law enforcement including decryption of encrypted communications, with Technical Capability Notices requiring systemic weaknesses.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application to encrypted communications [+1], criminal penalties for providers (failure to comply) [+1], forced decryption capabilities [+1], severe chilling effect on journalist and worker communications [+1], mission creep to 'serious crime' from terrorism [+1], limited judicial oversight (AG approval only) [+0.5], no digital exclusion but creates security exclusion [+0]. Total: 5.5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Active since 2018 with increased use reported 2024-2025. Section 317ZG prohibits 'systemic weaknesses' but technical experts agree compelled assistance creates de facto backdoors. ASIO reports 'fewer than 20' TARs issued, but secrecy provisions prevent accurate accounting. Five Eyes outlier - only Australia lacks judicial authorization for these powers. Used for 'serious crime' not just terrorism. Threatens global security by undermining encryption standards. Worker organizing vulnerable as secure communications compromised.
