# Digital ID and Authentication Council of Canada (DIACC) Expansion

## Pan-Canadian Trust Framework (PCTF) Verified Person Component

## Description

Expansion of Verified.Me and DIACC Pan-Canadian Trust Framework enabling private sector digital identity verification for legal, financial, and employment sectors, with federated but increasingly centralized biometric collection.

## Severity Rationale

> Voluntary currently but increasing pressure for adoption [+0.5], no criminal penalties [+0], biometric collection for higher assurance levels [+1], potential chilling effect on employment if adopted by gig platforms [+0.5], mission creep to banking and legal services explicit [+1], oversight by DIACC (industry body, not judicial) [+0.5], digital exclusion of those without bank accounts [+1]. Total: 4.5/7 → 3/5

## Implementation Notes

PCTF certification achieving uptake in legal sector (FCT Client ID Verification certified October 2025). Claims 'voluntary' and 'decentralized' but architecture enables mission creep toward mandatory employment verification. No federal legislation establishing framework - operates as industry self-regulation with government participation. Concerns about 'function creep' similar to UK Digital ID. British Columbia, Ontario, and Quebec provincial digital IDs being integrated. Worker surveillance risk as gig platforms (Uber, DoorDash) adopt for 'verification' creating permanent biometric employment records without labor protections.
