## Description

Government-issued digital identity card for smartphones, announced March 2025 for launch November 2026. Will complement physical eID cards, available via MyGov.be app. Valid for Schengen travel.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application (all ID card holders eligible) [+1], biometric collection (will include mobile driver license) [+0.5], mission creep to Schengen travel [+0.5], digital exclusion (smartphone required) [+1], no criminal penalties [0], voluntary (physical cards remain valid) [0]. Total: 3/5

## Implementation Notes

BELGIUM'S OFFICIAL EUDI WALLET IMPLEMENTATION. Key details: (1) Free for all physical ID card holders; (2) Available via MyGov.be app (launched May 2024); (3) Will include identity card, mobile driving license, European Health Insurance Card; (4) Valid for travel within Schengen zone; (5) Physical and digital systems will coexist per Interior Minister Bernard Quintin. Timeline: November 2026 launch. Security: Claims 'security by design,' cybersecurity prioritized, strict EU certification required. Concerns: (1) Centralized government database of biometric/identity data; (2) Smartphone dependency creates digital divide; (3) Mission creep to travel/documents creates tracking potential; (4) Integration with itsme creates duopoly control over identity; (5) 'Voluntary' but network effects will pressure adoption. Opposition: Cybersecurity expert Inti De Ceukelaire notes security 'need not pose problem' but civil liberties concerns about surveillance not addressed. No significant civil society opposition yet - early stage. Workers' concern: Potential for employer-mandated digital ID for workplace access, creating worker surveillance infrastructure.
