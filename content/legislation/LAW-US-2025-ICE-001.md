# ICE Social Media Surveillance Expansion

## ICE National Criminal Analysis and Targeting Center (NCATC) Expansion

## Description

ICE expansion of social media monitoring through contractor-operated 'intelligence fusion centers' in Vermont and California, specifically targeting immigrant workers for deportation enforcement using commercial databases and open-source intelligence.

## Severity Rationale

> Targets specific population (immigrants/visa holders) but affects entire population through chilling effect [+0.5], no criminal penalties for surveilled but detention/deportation [+1], irreversible data collection and retention [+1], explicit targeting of worker organizing among immigrant communities [+1], mission creep from 'national security' to general law enforcement [+1], no judicial oversight for open-source surveillance [+1], digital exclusion through fear of monitoring [+0.5]. Total: 5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Active procurement as of October 2025. 'Limited success' with current open-source monitoring driving demand for enhanced contractor capabilities. Targets Facebook, Instagram, X, and commercial databases. Explicitly generates leads for 'Worksite Enforcement Operations' targeting undocumented labor. Creates severe chilling effect on immigrant worker organizing - workers afraid to post about wage theft or unsafe conditions. Part of broader DHS 'border security' privatization. Contractors likely include Palantir and similar surveillance tech firms.
