# Digital ID for Employment (Britcard)

## Digital ID Scheme (announced under Immigration and Asylum Act 2025 provisions)

| Field | Value |

## Description

Mandatory digital ID cards required for all employment right-to-work checks by 2029, explicitly targeting undocumented workers and creating a two-tier labor market where migrant labor is hyper-surveilled while employers gain enhanced verification powers.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application for employment [+1], criminal penalties for non-compliance [+1], biometric collection mandated [+1], no judicial oversight for data sharing [+1], mission creep to housing and benefits explicit [+1], digital exclusion of undocumented migrants [+1]. Total: 6/7 → 5/5

## Implementation Notes

Faced massive opposition including 2.9 million signature petition; Labour government backed down on 'compulsory' language January 2026 but maintained mandatory requirements for employment. Opposition spans Corbyn-aligned Labour MPs, SNP, Lib Dems, and Northern Ireland parties. Compared to Blair's failed ID scheme but with enhanced biometric capabilities. Contractors likely include Oracle and Yoti. Creates severe chilling effect for union organizing among undocumented workers who will be excluded from formal employment entirely.
