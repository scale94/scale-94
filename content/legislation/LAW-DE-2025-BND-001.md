## Description

Comprehensive expansion of Federal Intelligence Service (BND) powers including full-content interception at internet exchange nodes, 6-month data retention, offensive hacking of foreign technology providers, and surveillance of foreign journalists in Germany.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application - traffic retention at internet nodes captures entire population [+1], criminal penalties for non-compliance by providers [+1], irreversible full-content interception and 6-month data retention [+1], explicit targeting of journalists and foreign nationals in Germany [+1], mission creep to 'offensive hacking' of foreign providers [+1], no judicial oversight for foreign intelligence collection [+1], creates digital exclusion by surveillance chilling effect [+0.5]. Total: 6.5/7 → 5/5

## Implementation Notes

Draft legislation aims to reduce reliance on US intelligence by expanding German mass surveillance capabilities to match UK GCHQ and French DGSE. 'Hack foreign providers' clause creates friction with US tech platforms (Google, Meta, X). Removes distinction between intelligence and law enforcement by allowing 'federal trojan' use. Part of post-war privacy erosion despite constitutional protections. History of data retention laws being struck down by Constitutional Court suggests this will face legal challenge if passed.
