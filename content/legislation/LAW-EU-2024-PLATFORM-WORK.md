# Platform Work Directive (Algorithmic Management Regulation)

## Directive (EU) 2024/2831 on improving working conditions in platform work

## Description

Regulates algorithmic management of platform workers including automated monitoring, performance evaluation, and decision-making. Establishes employment presumption and requires human oversight of algorithmic decisions affecting workers.

## Severity Rationale

> Worker surveillance provisions are significant [+1 for chilling effect on organizing], biometric collection prohibited except for authentication [partial +0.5], no universal application (only platform workers) [0], no criminal penalties for workers [0], some judicial oversight via human review requirements [0]. Mixed legislation - includes protections but normalizes surveillance infrastructure. Total: 3/5

## Implementation Notes

Mixed bag from workers' rights perspective. POSITIVE: (1) Rebuttable presumption of employment status; (2) Bans processing emotional/psychological state data, private conversations, trade union prediction data; (3) Requires human oversight with authority to override automated decisions; (4) Mandates algorithmic impact assessments every 2 years; (5) Protects oversight staff from retaliation. CONCERNING: (1) Normalizes pervasive worker surveillance as default; (2) 'Business users' exception may exclude many workers; (3) Member states have until December 2026 to implement - weak enforcement timeline; (4) Biometric data still allowed for authentication; (5) Location/behavior tracking still permitted during work. Union positions: Generally supportive but criticised as 'watered down'. ETUC (European Trade Union Confederation) campaigned for stronger provisions.
