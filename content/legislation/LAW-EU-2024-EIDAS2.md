# eIDAS 2.0 / European Digital Identity Wallet

## Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 amending Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS 2.0)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Location** | EU |
| **Jurisdiction** | Union |
| **Status** | IMPLEMENTING |
| **Severity** | 4/5 — HIGH |

## Description

Mandates EU member states issue digital identity wallets to citizens and residents for cross-border authentication. Contains controversial Article 45 requiring browsers to trust government-approved certificate authorities (QWACs), potentially enabling man-in-the-middle attacks and government interception. Creates centralized identity infrastructure linking health, finance, and travel data.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application for all EU citizens/residents who request wallets [+1], no direct criminal penalties but exclusion from services [+0.5], irreversible biometric collection for wallet authentication [+1], chilling effect through cross-sector activity linking [+1], mission creep to private sector services explicit in text [+1], government-controlled certificate authorities create surveillance risk [+0.5], digital exclusion for those without smartphones [+0.5]. Total: 4/5 (High)

## Implementation Notes

Adopted April 2024, full implementation by 2026. 500+ signatories on open letter expressing concerns about profiling and government interception. Article 45.2 mandates browsers trust government-designated CAs even if unsafe - browsers cannot unilaterally revoke. Creates 'digital sovereignty' framework that could enable mass surveillance. Pilot programs ongoing. Member states must provide wallets by 2026. Non-discrimination clause included but de facto mandatory through service provider adoption.
