# Digital Services Act (DSA)

## Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act)

## Description

Comprehensive regulation of online platforms requiring content moderation systems, transparency reports, risk assessments, and algorithmic accountability for Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs).

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application for VLOPs (45M+ users) [+1], data retention for content moderation [+0.5], platform mandated scanning for illegal content [+0.5], no criminal penalties for users [0], some judicial oversight via transparency requirements [0], mission creep to algorithmic accountability [+0.5]. Primarily corporate accountability with surveillance implications. Total: 3/5

## Implementation Notes

DSA is primarily corporate accountability legislation but has surveillance dimensions. VLOPs must: (1) Maintain content moderation systems scanning user content; (2) Retain data for transparency reporting; (3) Provide data access to 'vetted researchers' (potential surveillance vector); (4) Algorithmic transparency for recommender systems. Enforcement: Fines up to 6% global turnover. Active investigations against TikTok, AliExpress, Temu, Facebook, Instagram. From leftist perspective: Positive for platform accountability but creates surveillance infrastructure through mandatory content scanning. Workers' concern: Platform workers (content moderators) face traumatic working conditions; algorithmic management of gig workers intersects with DSA transparency requirements.
