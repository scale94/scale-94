# Communications (Retention of Data) Act 2011 (Section 3A Orders)

## Communications (Retention of Data) Act 2011 as amended by Criminal Justice (Mutual Assistance) (Amendment) Act 2022

## Description

Mass retention of telecommunications metadata (user, traffic, location data) by service providers for 12 months. Minister obtains High Court order under Section 3A claiming 'serious and genuine threat to state security.' Access requires District Court authorization.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application (all mobile/ISP users) [+1], no judicial oversight for retention (ministerial order, ex parte) [+1], irreversible data collection (metadata retained 12 months) [+1], mission creep from 'state security' to broader law enforcement [+0.5], digital exclusion not applicable [0]. Total: 3.5/5 rounded to 4/5

## Implementation Notes

IMPLEMENTS MASS SURVEILLANCE DESPITE CJEU RULINGS. Background: (1) 2006: DRI challenged data retention leading to CJEU Digital Rights Ireland ruling striking down EU Data Retention Directive; (2) 2022: Act amended after CJEU Tele2 ruling; (3) June 2023: First Section 3A order granted; (4) June 2024: Second order granted for 12 months. Current regime: (1) General and indiscriminate retention permitted only for 'state security'; (2) Minister applies ex parte to High Court judge designated by President of High Court; (3) Access to retained data requires District Court authorization; (4) Annual report to Taoiseach by designated High Court judge. Concerns: (1) 'State security' justification is catch-all - no transparency about specific threats; (2) Ex parte applications mean no opposing arguments heard; (3) Graham Dwyer case established CJEU limits but Ireland using 'state security' exception; (4) Identity of every person you phone/text/email retained with location data. Opposition: Digital Rights Ireland ongoing litigation to strike down national laws. Workers' concern: Mass surveillance of communications chills worker organizing, union communications, solidarity networks - metadata reveals relationships and meeting locations.
