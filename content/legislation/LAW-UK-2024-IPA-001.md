# Investigatory Powers Act 2024 Amendments

## Investigatory Powers (Amendment) Act 2024

## Description

Expands bulk personal dataset retention and adds new public authorities (including Intellectual Property Office and ambulance services) authorized to access communications data without warrant, with implementation commencing April-June 2025.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal data retention applies to entire population [+1], no criminal penalties for individuals but severe surveillance [+0], irreversible bulk personal dataset collection [+1], explicit targeting of journalists and organizers possible [+1], mission creep to new agencies (IPO, ambulance services) [+1], limited judicial oversight via 'double lock' but broad ministerial discretion [+0.5], no digital exclusion [+0]. Total: 4.5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Commenced April 25, 2025 for third-party bulk datasets; June 20, 2025 for communications data authorities. Expands warrantless access to subscriber information for copyright enforcement (IPO) and ambulance services. 'Double lock' authorization claims judicial oversight but Judicial Commissioners are former judges appointed by government. Union communications particularly vulnerable as 'economic well-being' justifies surveillance. Links to Renters' Rights Act 2025 investigatory powers for landlord enforcement.
