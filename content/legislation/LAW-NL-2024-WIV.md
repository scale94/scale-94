## Description

Dutch bulk surveillance law permitting untargeted interception of cable communications (internet traffic) by AIVD and MIVD. Ministerial authorization required, oversight by Review Committee on Intelligence and Security Services (CTIVD).

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application (bulk cable interception) [+1], no judicial oversight (ministerial authorization for bulk interception) [+1], irreversible data collection (untargeted interception and storage) [+1], mission creep from 'national security' to broader uses [+0.5], digital exclusion not applicable [0]. Total: 3.5/5 rounded to 4/5

## Implementation Notes

BULK SURVEILLANCE VIA CABLE INTERCEPTION. Key provisions: (1) 'Sleepnet' (dragnet) - bulk interception of cable communications; (2) Untargeted collection of internet traffic; (3) Minister of Interior authorizes bulk interception; (4) CTIVD (Review Committee) provides oversight; (5) Data shared with foreign intelligence services (NSA, etc.). History: (1) 2017: Wiv 2017 passed replacing Wiv 2002; (2) 2018: Advisory referendum rejected Wiv 2017 (49.4% turnout, majority against); (3) Government ignored referendum result; (4) 2024: Law amended but bulk interception retained. Current status: Active use by AIVD (domestic) and MIVD (military). Concerns: (1) Untargeted collection = mass surveillance; (2) Ministerial authorization not independent judicial oversight; (3) Data retention periods unclear; (4) Sharing with foreign agencies (Five Eyes); (5) No notification of affected individuals. Opposition: (1) 2018 referendum showed public opposition; (2) Privacy First and digital rights groups challenging; (3) EDRi documenting violations. Workers' concern: Bulk interception captures all worker communications, union organizing, strike planning - no protections for labor organizing activities.
