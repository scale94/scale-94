# Bill C-2 (Strong Borders Act) - Lawful Access

## Bill C-2, An Act respecting certain measures relating to the security of the border

## Description

Omnibus border security bill attempting to introduce warrantless access to subscriber data and IP addresses, forcing technical assistance from service providers, and expanding surveillance powers to CSIS and law enforcement under 'lawful access' framework.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application to 'any person providing services to the public' [+1], criminal penalties for non-compliance (fines up to $5,000, potential imprisonment) [+1], irreversible subscriber data and IP retention [+1], chilling effect on journalists and organizers [+1], mission creep to medical records and Canada Post mail [+1], warrantless access removes judicial oversight [+1], digital exclusion through fear of surveillance [+0.5]. Total: 5.5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Effectively withdrawn/replaced by Bill C-12 in October 2025 after massive opposition from OpenMedia, BCCLA, CIPPIC, and privacy experts. Would have allowed warrantless access to medical records, hotel bookings, and electronic communications. CSIS internal emails celebrated provisions as 'GREAT NEWS!!' Michael Geist called it 'unprecedented violation of Canadian privacy rights.' Would have enabled CLOUD Act compliance with US, exposing Canadian data to US law enforcement.
