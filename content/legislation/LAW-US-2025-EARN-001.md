# EARN IT Act (Federal Encryption Threat)

## Eliminating Abusive and Rampant Neglect of Interactive Technologies Act

| Field | Value |

## Description

Federal legislation threatening Section 230 liability protections for platforms offering end-to-end encryption unless they implement client-side scanning, effectively mandating encryption backdoors under the guise of combating CSAM.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application to all encrypted communications [+1], criminal liability for platform employees (Section 230 carveout) [+1], irreversible mandate for client-side scanning or backdoors [+1], severe chilling effect on organizing (workers cannot communicate securely) [+1], mission creep from CSAM to all 'illegal content' [+1], no judicial oversight for scanning mandates [+1], creates digital exclusion by making encrypted services unavailable [+1]. Total: 7/7 → 5/5

## Implementation Notes

Did not advance to full vote in 2023-2024 session but provisions reintroduced in subsequent sessions. Would force platforms to choose: abandon encryption or face state-level civil liability. Mozilla, EFF, Signal, WhatsApp opposed. Worker organizing particularly threatened as E2EE currently protects union communications from employer surveillance. If passed, would force labor organizers to use unencrypted channels or foreign platforms.
