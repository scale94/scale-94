# SREN Law / Digital Space Security and Regulation Law

## Loi n° 2024-449 visant à sécuriser et réguler l'espace numérique (SREN)

## Description

Mandates strict age verification for accessing pornographic content online, requiring 'double anonymity' solutions. ARCOM standard requires biometric age estimation or ID verification. Blocks sites that don't comply. Framework designed to expand to social media, online gambling, and alcohol sales. Eliminates online anonymity for accessing adult content.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application for internet users accessing regulated content [+1], criminal penalties up to €150,000 or 2% global turnover [+1], biometric age estimation and ID verification required [+1], chilling effect on access to information and sexual expression [+1], explicit mission creep to social media, gambling, alcohol [+1], ARCOM enforcement with limited judicial oversight [+0.5], digital exclusion for those without ID or banking [+0.5]. Total: 4/5 (High)

## Implementation Notes

Enacted May 21, 2024. Age verification obligations fully enforceable from January 2025. ARCOM standard published October 10, 2024 with 3-month compliance deadline (April 11, 2025). 'Double anonymity' principle: site doesn't know identity, verification provider doesn't know site visited. Biometric age estimation allowed. Non-compliant sites face blocking orders within 48 hours. CNIL previously recommended trusted third parties. Framework explicitly designed for expansion to other sectors. La Quadrature du Net and digital rights groups oppose as surveillance infrastructure.
