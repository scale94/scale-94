# Amazon Warehouse Worker Algorithmic Surveillance

## Description

Systematic surveillance of warehouse workers through barcode badge tracking, 'scan to scan' productivity metrics, and algorithmic management systems that automatically terminate workers failing to meet quotas, explicitly used to prevent union formation.

## Severity Rationale

> Applies to specific workforce (warehouse/gig workers) not entire population [+0], no criminal penalties but termination/blacklisting [+0.5], biometric badge data collection potentially permanent [+1], explicit chilling effect on union organizing [+1], mission creep to 'productivity optimization' [+0.5], no judicial oversight for workplace surveillance [+1], digital exclusion through algorithmic management [+0.5]. Total: 4.5/7 → 3/5

## Implementation Notes

Active implementation across Amazon fulfillment network. Barcode badges track location, productivity, and 'time off task.' Algorithmic termination systems operate without human review. Research shows workers understand badges as identity and surveillance mechanism: 'Your badge is everything... that's how they track you.' Creates panopticon effect preventing union organizing. Similar systems deployed at UPS, FedEx, and gig platforms (Uber, DoorDash). No federal legislation protecting workers from algorithmic management. NLRB has challenged some implementations but protections weak.
