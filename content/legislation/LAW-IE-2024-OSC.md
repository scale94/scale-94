# Online Safety Code (Age Verification Mandate)

## Online Safety Code 2024 (under Online Safety and Media Regulation Act 2022)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| **Jurisdiction** | National |
| **Status** | IMPLEMENTING |
| **Severity** | 4/5 — HIGH |

## Description

Mandatory age verification for video-sharing platforms headquartered in Ireland (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, etc.). Requires 'effective age assurance' for adult content - self-declaration not sufficient. Part A active from November 2024, Part B (age verification) from July 2025.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application for designated platform users [+1], criminal penalties for platforms (fines up to EUR 20M or 10% revenue) [+1], biometric/ID document collection for age verification [+1], digital exclusion (requires ID documents/smartphone) [+1], no judicial oversight (platforms decide verification methods) [+1], mission creep from pornography to 'harmful content' [+0.5]. Total exceeds 5 - 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Major civil liberties concerns: (1) Mandatory age verification requires government ID or biometric facial recognition - creates surveillance infrastructure; (2) Platforms designated: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, Tumblr, Reddit (Reddit de-designated May 2025); (3) 'Adult-only content' definition vague - platforms decide what requires verification; (4) Children's data collected for age verification cannot be used for commercial purposes BUT retention periods unclear; (5) No clear standards for age assurance - platforms can choose credit cards, AI facial analysis, behavioral profiling; (6) 9-month implementation delay (July 2025) left children unprotected per Children's Rights Alliance. Opposition: Tumblr and Reddit took unsuccessful High Court actions. Privacy campaigners concerned about sensitive data storage. Digital Rights Ireland monitoring implementation. Workers' concern: Platform workers (content moderators) face increased exposure to traumatic content; age verification creates barriers to anonymous worker organizing online.
