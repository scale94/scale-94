# Digital Personalausweis Online Expansion

## Neue Personalausweis (nPA) Digital Wallet Expansion

## Description

Nationwide rollout of smartphone-based digital ID cards allowing biometric verification for employment, banking, and healthcare, integrated with EU Digital Identity Wallet framework requiring implementation by 2027.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal assignment of digital identity to all citizens [+1], no criminal penalties yet but administrative penalties likely [+0.5], biometric collection (facial recognition for smartphone storage) [+1], potential chilling effect if linked to employment [+0.5], explicit mission creep to EU Digital Identity Wallet framework [+1], judicial oversight for data access unclear [+0.5], digital exclusion of those without smartphones [+1]. Total: 4.5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

Voluntary in theory but 'every citizen will be assigned a digital identity.' Coalition government claims alignment with EU eIDAS regulation. Involves Google and Samsung in wallet development. Connects to BundID and BundesIdent previous systems criticized for low adoption due to complexity. Explicitly designed to enable employment verification, raising concerns about eventual mandatory use for work following UK model. EU interoperability means data sharing across member states with varying privacy protections.
