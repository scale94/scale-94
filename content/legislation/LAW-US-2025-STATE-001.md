## Description

Patchwork of state laws mandating government ID or biometric age verification for social media access, with Texas, Louisiana, and others actively enforcing while courts issue preliminary injunctions in Arkansas, Ohio, and California.

## Severity Rationale

> Universal application to all social media users in affected states [+1], criminal/civil penalties for platforms [+1], biometric and government ID data collection [+1], chilling effect on youth organizing and political speech [+1], mission creep from 'protecting minors' to general identity verification [+1], no judicial oversight for data sharing with law enforcement [+0.5], digital exclusion for unbanked/undocumented [+1]. Total: 5.5/7 → 4/5

## Implementation Notes

50-state patchwork creates compliance nightmare. Texas App Store Accountability Act enjoined December 2025; Louisiana Act 456 permanently enjoined December 2025 but Act 481 remains effective July 2026. NetChoice (industry group) challenging on First Amendment grounds with mixed success. Creates dual-class internet: verified (ID holders) and unverified (excluded). Disproportionately impacts undocumented workers, youth organizers, and low-income communities without government ID. Data likely shared with ICE/DHS given lack of privacy protections in most state laws.
